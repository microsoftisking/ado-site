Welcome to the Show
Ground Rules · ルール · Read before entering

Hey! Before you grab your pass, let's lay down the ground rules.

ADO is a private API gateway built exclusively for members of our Discord server. Do not share your personal key or the endpoint URL with anyone outside the server.

Because AI roleplay can get unpredictable, ADO is strictly an 18+ service. We cannot verify the age of strangers online, which is exactly why sharing your access is strictly forbidden. We are not liable for the content you generate, or for any harm caused if you leak access to outsiders.

By clicking accept, you confirm that you are:

✦ 18 years of age or older
✦ An active member of our Discord server
✦ Committed to keeping ADO safe and private for the community

Break the rules, and your key gets permanently nuked. Thanks for keeping it local! 💜

🔒
ADO Privacy Promise
No spying. Ever. · プライバシー保護

Let's be real: nobody wants their roleplays read by an admin. So here's our privacy guarantee:

✦ We do not log, read, or store your chat histories, character cards, or prompts.
✦ The upstream AI provider is completely blind to who you are.
✦ The only data our system tracks is your account ID and daily request count to ensure nobody drains the master key.

What you RP stays entirely between you and the AI. Your secrets are safe here.

This is a community promise, not a legal document. ADO is a free, volunteer-run service. Use it responsibly.
